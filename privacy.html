Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version. Jiggy Teenz is a fitness and social development programme for teenagers aged 14–18 in the UK. We collect only what we need to run sessions safely. We never sell your data. There are no adverts. There is no open chat between members. You or your parent/guardian can ask us to delete everything at any time, and we will.

1. Who we are

Jiggy Teenz ("we", "us") provides coached fitness sessions and a companion mobile app for members aged 14–18. For data protection purposes we are the data controller.

Contact: info@jiggyteenz.co.uk · jiggyteenz.co.uk

2. Because our members are children

Everyone using the Jiggy Teenz app is under 18, so we follow the UK GDPR and the Information Commissioner's Age Appropriate Design Code. In practice that means:

  • A parent or guardian must approve the account by email before it becomes active.
  • We collect the minimum information needed, and nothing "just in case".
  • Settings default to the most private option.
  • There are no adverts, no tracking for advertising, and no profiling.
  • There is no open chat, no public profiles and no way for members to message each other.
  • We never use nudge techniques to push spending. Coins are earned by attending sessions; buying them is optional and kept in one clearly labelled place.

What we do NOT collect

  • No card or bank details — payments are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our systems.
  • No photographs of members in the app. Avatars are illustrated characters only.
  • No location tracking, no contacts, no microphone, no advertising identifiers.

4. Health information

Members may optionally add a note for coaches, such as "asthma inhaler in bag" or "recovering from an ankle sprain". This is voluntary, visible only to Jiggy Teenz coaches and admin staff, and used solely to keep the member safe during a session. It can be edited or removed at any time in the app under Profile → Safety.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while the membership is active, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Attendance records: up to 3 years, as a safeguarding and insurance record of who attended which session.
  • Payment records: 6 years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Support messages: 12 months.

Where we must keep a record after deletion (for example a payment for tax purposes), it is reduced to the minimum and detached from the member's profile.

7. Your rights

Members and their parents or guardians can ask us to:

  • see a copy of the information we hold;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete the account and its data;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw the guardian's approval, which closes the account.

The quickest route is in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account, which removes everything immediately. You can also request deletion from this website without installing the app, or email info@jiggyteenz.co.uk. We respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Keeping it safe

  • All data is encrypted in transit.
  • Members can only ever read their own records; this is enforced by the database, not just the app.
  • Coin balances, bookings and payments are decided on our server, so they cannot be altered from a phone.
  • Card payments are verified cryptographically before anything is credited.
  • Only named Jiggy Teenz staff can see member records, and only what their role requires.

9. Cookies

The Jiggy Teenz app does not use cookies or advertising trackers. It stores your login on your own device so you do not have to sign in every time. This website uses no tracking cookies.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for anything significant, tell members and guardians by email.

Questions? Email info@jiggyteenz.co.uk and a real person will reply. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about your child's account, say so in the subject line and we will prioritise it.
