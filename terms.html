Terms of Service

Last updated: 2 September 2026 · Version 1.0

In plain English. Jiggy Teenz is for 14–18 year olds. A parent or guardian has to approve the account. Be kind, train safely, and listen to your coach. Jiggy Coins are a loyalty reward, not money. Weekly passes cover three sessions and expire at the end of the week.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms are an agreement between Jiggy Teenz and the member using the app, together with the parent or guardian who approves the account. By creating an account and ticking the box in the app, both agree to these terms.

2. Joining

  • Membership is open to young people aged 14–18.
  • A parent or guardian must approve the account by clicking the link we email them. Until they do, the member can look around but cannot book sessions.
  • The information given at sign-up must be accurate, including age and the guardian's email address.
  • One account per person. Accounts must not be shared or sold.

3. Your account

Keep your password to yourself and tell us straight away if you think someone else has got into your account. You are responsible for what happens on your account, though we will always be fair if something has clearly gone wrong.

4. Behaviour we expect

The Jiggy Teenz code is Respect, Discipline, Teamwork and Positivity. Concretely:

  • Treat every member, coach and staff member as you would want to be treated.
  • Bullying, harassment, discrimination or threatening behaviour ends a membership immediately.
  • Follow your coach's instructions at all times during a session.
  • Tell a coach about any injury, health condition, or if you feel unwell. Never push through pain.

5. Sessions, passes and booking

  • A weekly pass covers three sessions within that week.
  • Unused sessions expire at the end of the week. They do not roll over and are not refunded.
  • Every new member gets 100 Jiggy Coins free, once, which covers a first week.
  • After that a pass costs 99 Jiggy Coins or £9.99 by card.
  • Please only book sessions you intend to attend. Repeatedly booking and not turning up may cost you booking privileges, because it takes a place from someone else.
  • Cancel in the app if your plans change and the session returns to your pass.
  • We may occasionally have to move or cancel a session. If we cancel, that session goes back on your pass.

6. Jiggy Coins

  • Coins are a loyalty reward, not money. They have no cash value.
  • They cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred between members, or withdrawn.
  • Inside the app, 100 Jiggy Coins represents £10 of value.
  • Coins are earned by attending sessions, keeping streaks, reaching milestones, attending meetups and referring friends. They can also be bought, but nobody has to buy them.
  • If a membership ends, any remaining coins are lost.
  • We may adjust earning rates in future. We will tell members before we do.
  • Coins gained through a fault, bug or dishonesty may be removed.

7. Payments

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card details.
  • Purchases over £10 require guardian approval.
  • Because a pass gives immediate access to sessions, it is not refundable once used. If something has gone wrong, email us and we will sort it out fairly.
  • Merchandise is handed over in person at a session, using the code shown in the app.

8. Gym Buddy

Members can pair up as Gym Buddies to encourage each other. To be clear: buddies encourage, they do not coach. Anything about safety, injuries or wellbeing goes to a Jiggy Teenz coach, never to another member.

9. Photos and media

We sometimes photograph or film sessions for our website and social media. Tell a coach if you would rather not appear and we will respect that, no questions asked. Members do not upload photographs in the app.

10. Health and safety

Important. Jiggy Teenz sessions are physical exercise. If a member has a medical condition, injury, or any doubt about taking part, please speak to a doctor first. Our coaches are qualified to lead training, but we are not medical professionals and the app does not give medical advice. In an emergency we will call the emergency services and the guardian on the account.

11. Ending a membership

  • A member or their guardian can leave at any time — in the app under Profile → Settings, or by emailing us.
  • We may suspend or end an account that breaks these terms, puts others at risk, or attempts to cheat the coins or booking system.
  • If we end an account for a serious breach, coins and passes are forfeited.

12. If something goes wrong

We do our best to keep the app running, but we cannot promise it will never be unavailable. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.

13. Changes

If we change these terms we will update the date above and tell members and guardians about anything significant by email.

Anything unclear? Email info@jiggyteenz.co.uk. We would much rather explain something than have a member or parent unsure where they stand.
