Delete a Jiggy Teenz account

Members and their parents or guardians can have an account and all its data removed. No reason needed, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

Quickest way — from inside the app. Open Jiggy Teenz → tap your avatar → Settings → Delete my account → type DELETE. It happens straight away.

Or request it here

Use this form if you cannot get into the app, or if you are a parent or guardian requesting deletion on your child's behalf. It opens an email to us with the details filled in.

This opens your email app with the request ready to send. If nothing happens, email info@jiggyteenz.co.uk with the details above.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to the account is removed permanently:

  • Name, email, age and guardian email address
  • Emergency contact and any note left for coaches
  • Jiggy Coins balance and history
  • Bookings, attendance record and streaks
  • Avatar, titles and any rewards
  • Support messages

What we have to keep, and why

  • Payment records for 6 years, because UK tax law requires it. These are reduced to the amount, date and reference, and detached from the member's profile.
  • Attendance dates for up to 3 years where needed as a safeguarding and insurance record of who was present at a session. These are anonymised where we can.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Requests made through this page are actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We will email you to confirm once it is done.

Just want to pause instead? You do not have to delete anything to take a break — simply stop booking, and your coins and streak will be waiting. Email info@jiggyteenz.co.uk if you would like a hand.
